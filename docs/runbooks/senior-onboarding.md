# Senior Contributor Onboarding Guide

Welcome to your class memory archive! **The Capsule** is a private, accountless digital yearbook built exclusively for your graduating class.

---

## 🚀 How to Enter & Contribute

1. **Get the Code:** Obtain the active 8-character class access code from your Senior Admin.
2. **Enter the Capsule:**
   - Open your class capsule web link on your mobile phone or browser.
   - Enter the access code on the landing page keypad.
3. **Explore Memories:**
   - Browse the **Timeline** for chronological moments.
   - View the **Gallery** for candid photos and videos.
   - Read **Senior Memories** and **Class Messages**.
4. **Add a Memory:**
   - Tap **Add Memory** at the top of the collection screen.
   - Select content type (*Photo*, *Video*, *Written Memory*, or *Class Message*).
   - Drop your photo/video or write your story.
   - Enter your display name (e.g., *"Jordan M."*).
   - Review and share with your class!

---

## 🔒 Privacy Notice

- **No User Accounts:** You do not need to create an account, password, or profile.
- **Permanent Submissions:** Submissions are shared directly with your class and cannot be edited after posting. Please check your submission carefully before confirming.
- **EXIF Stripping:** GPS location and camera data are automatically removed from photos upon upload.
